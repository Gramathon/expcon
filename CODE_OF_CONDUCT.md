# Code of Conduct

EXPCON aims to provide a respectful, constructive space for people with different
backgrounds and levels of experience. This policy applies to project issues,
pull requests, discussions, and other project spaces.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate, patient, and respectful.
- Discuss technical disagreements about ideas and evidence without attacking
  people.
- Give and receive review feedback in good faith.
- Respect privacy, confidentiality, and dataset or licensing restrictions.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, or threats.
- Deliberate intimidation, doxxing, or unwanted disclosure of private information.
- Persistent disruption of technical discussions or review processes.
- Sharing credentials, private data, or other material without permission.

Project maintainers may edit or remove content, close discussions, or restrict
participation when needed to keep the project safe and constructive.

## Reporting

For a conduct concern, contact the maintainer privately using the contact method
listed on the [@Gramathon GitHub profile](https://github.com/Gramathon). Do not put
personal or sensitive details in a public issue. Reports will be handled as
confidentially as the circumstances allow. The project does not promise a fixed
response time.
